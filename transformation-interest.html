01

Your weight has changed

The kilos — especially around your belly — seem harder to lose, even though you're eating better and exercising.

02

03

Your sleep may be disrupted, your periods may be changing, and perimenopause or menopause seems to have changed the rules.

04

You keep starting over

You start a diet. You exercise for a while. Life gets busy. And somehow you find yourself back at the beginning again.

05

You know what to do — but doing it consistently is hard

It's not a lack of information. You need something that works with your actual life, not another plan that works only when everything goes perfectly.

06

You want to feel like yourself again

Stronger. More energetic. More confident around food. And finally in control of your health without constantly having to start again.

If you recognised yourself in even a few of these, you're in the right place.
