Sustainable fat loss12 kg

Small, realistic changes helped her move beyond the belief that the gym alone was the answer.

Parvathy’s journey

12 kg down—and a completely different understanding of health.

“The journey was enriching—not just for achieving the goal, but for understanding the importance of sleep, healthy food and movement.”

Where she began

Parvathy believed that only intense gym workouts could create meaningful weight loss.

What changed

  • Made practical changes to everyday food habits
  • Understood the role of sleep, nutrition and movement together
  • Stayed supported through patience, encouragement and consistent guidance
THE RESULT

She lost 12 kg over six months and described finally achieving her long-held goal of getting into a healthier shape.

Energy & wellbeing10 kg

Nandhini Navneeth’s journey

10 kg lighter, with fewer migraines and far more energy.

“I feel less exhausted and more energetic to handle my hectic day.”

Where she began

Her results fluctuated, and weight returned whenever she took a break from exercise.

What changed

  • Moved away from crash diets toward balanced nutrition
  • Used a plan designed around her existing routine
  • Received day-to-day monitoring, motivation and reassurance
THE RESULT

Along with losing 10 kg, Nandhini reported drastically reduced migraines and body pain, better mental wellbeing and improved energy.

Revathy’s journey

From repeated regain to habits she could actually sustain.

“Her transformation has been nothing less than amazing.”

Where she began

Despite diets and workout routines, Revathy struggled to retain her weight loss.

What changed

  • Planned and prepared balanced meals with all food groups
  • Added three days of strength training
  • Spaced workouts for better recovery and practised moderation
THE RESULT

Her sweet cravings reduced, energy improved, and she achieved meaningful inch loss while building a more sustainable routine.

Vaishnavi’s journey

Lighter, healthier and more confident—without being forced onto a rigid diet.

“I feel lighter, happier and healthier now.”

Where she began

As a first-time nutrition coaching client, she worried that she would be forced to follow a restrictive plan.

Kavitha’s journey

Better energy, fewer cravings and a smoother menopause journey.

“Sushma was the pillar of support during my toughest time.”

Where she began

Despite regular walks and exercise, stubborn weight felt impossible to shift, alongside cravings and menopause-related discomfort.

What changed

  • Learned how to balance protein, carbohydrates and fats
  • Stopped midnight cravings and bingeing
  • Added movement she could enjoy, including longer walks and Zumba
THE RESULT

Kavitha reported steady weight loss, greater freshness and stamina, reduced back pain, and relief from previous sweating, breathlessness and panic episodes.

Lifestyle transformation9.7 kg

Anupama’s journey

9.7 kg in five months—and a routine she wanted to keep.

“I started with the aim to lose a few kilos, but it has now turned into a lifestyle routine.”

Where she began

Consistent workouts and diets had not helped her reach her pre-wedding goal, alongside PCOS concerns, bloating, low vitamin D and lethargy.

What changed

  • Used personalised nutrition, workout and progress tracking
  • Focused on muscle building and inch loss—not only scale weight
  • Received proactive accountability and support across time zones
THE RESULT

Anupama moved from 75 kg to 65.3 kg and described feeling better both physically and mentally.

Deepa’s journey

She ate familiar foods, ran better and stopped fearing a “diet”.

“I ate everything and yet lost weight.”

Where she began

Although active and fitness-focused, weight loss was not happening and she worried coaching might mean giving up foods she loved.

What changed

  • Learned the value of balanced meals without separate cooking
  • Limited binge eating while continuing to enjoy familiar foods
  • Improved running and maintained steady energy through the day
THE RESULT

Deepa reported weight loss, better running, sustained energy and a healthier relationship with food and daily life.

Arifa’s journey

From crash diets to a balanced lifestyle she understood and enjoyed.

“Weight loss is possible by eating foods that I love—not by starving myself.”

Where she began

Arifa had relied on crash diets and wanted a healthier, more sustainable way forward.

What changed
