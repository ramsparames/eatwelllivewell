Hi, I’m Sushma

Meet your coach

I understand because I’ve been there too.

I’m Sushma—a Certified Menopause Coaching Specialist, Precision Nutrition Level 2 Master Health Coach and INFS Certified Metabolic Health Coach.

01

I spent years putting myself last.

After more than two decades in the IT industry, balancing work, family and responsibilities, I realised my own wellbeing had quietly moved to the bottom of the list.

02

Strength training changed how I saw myself.

It rebuilt my confidence and energy—but also showed me that exercise alone wasn’t enough. Nutrition, recovery, habits and emotional wellbeing mattered too.

03

Then perimenopause changed the rules.

Despite exercising and eating well, I experienced weight gain, poor sleep and low energy. Understanding what was happening changed the direction of my work.

04

That experience became NourisHer.

Today I help women over 35 work with their changing bodies through practical, personalised coaching that supports sustainable fat loss, metabolic health, strength and confidence.
