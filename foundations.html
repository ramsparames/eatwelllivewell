Why Foundations

You probably do not need another diet. You need skills you can keep using.

Many women know the basics of eating well and moving more. The difficult part is making those choices work consistently through work, family, travel, social occasions and the changes that come after 40.

01

Know what matters

Understand the fundamentals of nutrition, movement, strength, sleep and recovery without getting lost in conflicting advice.

02

Turn knowledge into action

Practise a small number of meaningful habits until they begin to feel like part of normal life.

03

Learn to adapt

Build strategies for busy weeks, eating out, travel and imperfect days so one difficult week does not become a full restart.

How you are supported

Enough support to help you follow through, while still learning to do this for yourself.

Foundations combines group learning, guided strength work and regular individual conversations so you can understand the method and apply it to your own life.

Weekly group coaching

A live 60 minute session with Sushma to learn the core idea, talk through real situations and decide what to practise.

Two strength sessions each week

Guided sessions that help you learn the foundations of strength and build confidence through progressive practice.

Individual coaching every two weeks

Regular check ins with Sushma to review your progress, identify barriers and help you apply the programme in your life.

Eight cumulative habits

Two actions are introduced every two weeks until you have eight. Then the focus shifts from adding more to making those habits work in real life.

The 16 week journey

Build. Practise. Adapt. Own.

The programme moves in four clear phases. You first learn what matters, then practise it, adapt it to real life and finally turn it into a system you can continue using on your own.

Weeks 1 to 4

Build

Understand the fundamentals and begin building the first habits around food, movement and strength.

Weeks 5 to 8

Practise

Move from knowing to doing. Build the full set of eight habits and learn how consistency actually feels.

Weeks 9 to 12

Adapt

Learn how your habits can survive busy weeks, social occasions, poor sleep, stress and changing routines.

Weeks 13 to 16

Own

Turn what you have learned into your own system so you know what to do even when Sushma is not checking in.

What you leave with

Your own NourisHer system.

By the end, the aim is that you can build balanced meals, manage protein, move and strength train consistently, plan for busy periods, recover from difficult weeks and continue with your own eight sustainable habits.
