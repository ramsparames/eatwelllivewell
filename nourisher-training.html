Anupama · 9.7 kg

“I started with the aim to lose a few kilos, but it has now turned into a lifestyle routine.”
Deepa

A few common questions

Before you decide what to do next

Do I have to follow a strict diet?

No. NourisHer is built around balanced eating and familiar food rather than a rigid diet.

Is NourisHer only for women in menopause?

NourisHer is designed for women 40+ and can support women through perimenopause, menopause and the years around it.

Do I need to already be exercising?

No. The approach starts from where you are and builds movement and strength gradually.

Will I have to cook separate meals for my family?

No. The goal is to help you make changes that can work with your family meals and your real life.

What happens if I want to take the next step?
