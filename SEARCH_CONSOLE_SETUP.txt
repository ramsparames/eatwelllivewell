DEPLOY
------
Put sitemap.xml and robots.txt at the website root so these resolve to:
https://eatwelllivewell.in/sitemap.xml
https://eatwelllivewell.in/robots.txt

SEARCH CONSOLE
--------------
1. Open Google Search Console.
2. Add/choose the domain property for eatwelllivewell.in.
3. Verify ownership if it is not already verified.
4. Open Sitemaps.
5. Enter:
   sitemap.xml
6. Submit.
7. Use URL Inspection for each new indexable URL:
   /menopause-coach-india
   /belly-fat-after-40
8. Click Request Indexing for each page after confirming Google can access it.
9. Later, monitor Pages > Indexing and Search results > Performance.

IMPORTANT
---------
A sitemap helps discovery; it does not guarantee indexing or ranking.
Google recommends submitting a sitemap and using URL Inspection after new
pages are deployed. Google also notes that crawling/indexing can take time.

BEFORE DEPLOYMENT
-----------------
If your production site has additional public pages that should be indexed
(e.g. About, Success Stories, Privacy Policy, Terms), add their canonical
URLs to the sitemap before finalizing it. I have intentionally not invented
those URLs here.
