Is Transformation right for you?

This is for women who are ready for personal support—not another generic reset.

You do not need to be perfect or highly motivated. But you should be willing to participate honestly, practise the process and invest in long-term change.

Transformation may be right for you if…

  • You have tried multiple diets or plans but struggle to sustain them.
  • You want individual feedback rather than general advice.
  • You want accountability that adjusts with your real life.
  • You are navigating perimenopause, menopause or metabolic changes.
  • You want to become stronger, healthier and more confident—not simply lighter.

Frequently asked questions

What women usually want to know.

Is this only for women in menopause?

No. Transformation is designed for women 35+ navigating changing energy, body composition, recovery, habits and confidence, including perimenopause and menopause.

Will I have to follow a strict diet?

No. The approach is built around balanced, realistic eating and your own food preferences. The aim is sustainability, not restriction.

Do I need to exercise already?

No. Movement is personalised to your current ability and capacity. You do not need to be fit before beginning.

Can I start with Foundations instead?

Yes. Foundations is ideal when you want group support, structure and an accessible introduction to the NourisHer method before deciding on 1:1 coaching.

How do I know whether I’m accepted?

Sushma personally reviews each application. If Transformation appears suitable, you will be invited to a free introductory conversation before deciding.
